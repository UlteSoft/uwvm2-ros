## Authority, ownership and unknown outcomes

Each topic, send, feedback, result, cancel, Spot service, stop and lease-return
role has its own real Runtime grant. Native identity, canonical identity, complete
type graph, resource, QoS, deadline and finite quota must all be independently
proved through the actual Host/Gateway. No cancel-any, power, estop, graph,
policy/update/debug or raw socket permission is implied. Spot's Native operator
allowlist and controlled ROS network are additional boundaries, not replacements
for per-Instance Runtime authority. Its Native lease/history is shared, without
authenticated per-Guest isolation.

Guest history is finite and non-reusing. Full unsignedu64 goal IDs remain distinct
from local index, native ROS UUID/sequence and Spot command IDs. Zero/duplicate IDs
are refused. Lost replies retain unknown state and never authorize replay. Full
correlation/status/body validation precedes public typed assignment. Cancellation
acceptance does not finish a goal: inspect the real result and independent
telemetry. Legacy Action receives have no Guest ACK transaction; a decode failure
does not prove a native take was rolled back.

Borrowed owners are never secretly closed. Callers close every genuinely owned
raw handle independently and preserve all cleanup errors. Stop/ReturnLease are
independent authorized business calls, not inverse rollback, cancellation of an
uncertain operation or proof of physical stop. Source/session retirement and
a finite external supervision plan remain separate obligations.
